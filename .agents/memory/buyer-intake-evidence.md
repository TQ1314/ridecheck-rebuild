---
name: Buyer intake evidence retention
description: Keep listing-origin claims separate from inspection observations and avoid an implicit image-retention policy.
---

Treat extracted listing information as a buyer-confirmed claim, never as RideCheck-verified vehicle evidence. Keep the original claim and buyer correction distinguishable so later observations can be compared without overwriting either.

**Why:** Intake photos and screenshots may be the only evidence of what a listing said. Deleting them automatically or treating extracted facts as verified would make later mileage/VIN comparisons misleading. No canonical retention policy was established during the intake implementation.

**How to apply:** Any future cleanup or order-attachment work needs an explicit, agreed retention and access lifecycle. Until then, keep source material private and scoped, report the unresolved lifecycle, and do not claim an automatic purge exists.
