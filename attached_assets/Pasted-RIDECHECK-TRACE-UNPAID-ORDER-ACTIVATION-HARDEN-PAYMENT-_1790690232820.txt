PHASE 2 — DEFINE THE REQUIRED STATE MODEL

RideCheck payment policy:

PAYMENT FIRST BEFORE OPERATIONAL WORK.

An order/request may be persisted before payment if technically necessary, but it must remain non-operational.

Suggested distinction:

pending_payment
→ payment_received
→ operational workflow

An unpaid/pending order MUST NOT:

- become an actionable New Ops order
- initiate seller coordination
- become eligible for RC assignment/broadcast
- represent itself to Ops as ready for fulfillment
- generate language implying that a paid inspection has been confirmed

Only one of these may activate fulfillment:

A. verified successful Stripe server-side payment event
OR
B. explicit authorized Ops Lead override

Never trust the browser redirect alone as proof of payment.

Preserve existing legitimate unpaid/pending records. Do not destructively migrate historical orders merely to make statuses look clean.
