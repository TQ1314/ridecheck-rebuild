RIDECHECK BUYER BOOKING EXPERIENCE AUDIT — READ ONLY

Do not modify code, database schemas, migrations, UI, configuration, or data.

OBJECTIVE

Audit the CURRENT RideCheck buyer booking experience exactly as it exists today.

We are preparing to simplify vehicle intake and eventually add AI-assisted autofill.

Before building anything, determine:

1. What RideCheck already collects
2. Why each field exists
3. Which fields are actually required by backend/business logic
4. Which fields are duplicated
5. Which information could be derived instead of asking the buyer
6. Which information can be collected later from the seller or RideChecker
7. Which fields create unnecessary buyer friction
8. What existing listing/photo/VIN/extraction functionality already exists
9. What must NOT be broken when simplifying the experience

IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

RideCheck exists to help someone who found a used vehicle they may want to buy.

The vehicle may come from:
- Facebook Marketplace
- OfferUp
- Craigslist
- Autotrader
- Cars.com
- another used-car website
- dealership website
- dealership lot
- private seller
- roadside / FOR SALE sign
- auction
- friend/referral
- another source

The buyer should NOT have to understand RideCheck's internal workflow.

Long forms should not exist merely because the database has fields.

Do not recommend collecting information from the buyer unless RideCheck actually needs the buyer to provide it at that stage.

TARGET EXPERIENCE

Conceptually, RideCheck should eventually be able to say:

"Found a vehicle you want inspected?
Send us whatever you have."

Buyer can provide one or more of:

- listing URL
- screenshots
- vehicle/listing photos
- VIN
- basic vehicle details
- seller contact information

RideCheck extracts/derives what it safely can.

Buyer confirms the vehicle.

RideCheck handles the rest through the existing payment, seller coordination, assignment, inspection and report workflow.

Manual entry must remain available as fallback.

AI/extraction failure must never prevent booking.
