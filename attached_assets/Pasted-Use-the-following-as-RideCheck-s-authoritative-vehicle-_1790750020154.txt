RIDECHECK PRICING

STANDARD — $139
Regular gasoline vehicles that do not meet a Plus or Premium/Exotic criterion.

PLUS — $169
European vehicles, Hybrid vehicles, EVs, Diesel vehicles, and Heavy-Duty vehicles.

PREMIUM / EXOTIC — $299+
Exotic, Performance, and Collector vehicles.

Apply the highest applicable tier:

EDGE-CASE DECISIONS

1. ORDINARY VEHICLE PRICED $60,000+
Do NOT classify a vehicle as Premium/Exotic solely because of asking price.
Price alone is not a classification trigger.

2. OLD OR HIGH-MILEAGE BMW / EV / OTHER PLUS VEHICLE
Keep it Plus.
Age, mileage, depreciation, or low asking price must NEVER downgrade a vehicle from its appropriate package.

3. ORDINARY GASOLINE THREE-ROW SUVs
Vehicles such as ordinary gasoline Toyota Highlander, Honda Pilot, Chevrolet Tahoe, etc. are Standard unless another characteristic independently qualifies them for Plus or Premium/Exotic.
Three-row seating or large-SUV status alone is not a Plus trigger.

4. MERCEDES S-CLASS / MAYBACH
Plus by default because they are European vehicles.
Do not classify them as Premium/Exotic merely because they are expensive or luxury/flagship vehicles.
A specific performance, exotic, or collector variant may qualify for Premium/Exotic.

5. EUROPEAN MAKES
European vehicles are Plus by default.
Do not limit this rule to BMW, Mercedes-Benz, or Audi.
This includes makes such as Volkswagen, MINI, Volvo, Porsche, etc., unless a specific vehicle qualifies for Premium/Exotic.

6. PORSCHE 911
Classify Porsche 911, including base 911 variants, as Premium/Exotic because it is a performance sports car.
Performance variants such as Porsche GT/Turbo remain Premium/Exotic.
Ordinary non-performance Porsche SUVs such as standard Macan/Cayenne may remain Plus.

PERFORMANCE EXAMPLES

Vehicles that clearly qualify as performance vehicles — such as appropriate BMW M models, Mercedes-AMG performance models, Porsche 911/GT/Turbo models, and equivalent performance vehicles — should be Premium/Exotic.

IMPORTANT CLASSIFICATION PRINCIPLES

- Luxury brand alone does NOT mean Premium/Exotic.
- Flagship designation alone does NOT mean Premium/Exotic.
- Asking price alone does NOT mean Premium/Exotic.
- Age or mileage must NEVER downgrade a package.
- Additional vehicle complexity may move a vehicle upward, never downward.
- If a vehicle is genuinely ambiguous, surface it for a business decision rather than guessing.
