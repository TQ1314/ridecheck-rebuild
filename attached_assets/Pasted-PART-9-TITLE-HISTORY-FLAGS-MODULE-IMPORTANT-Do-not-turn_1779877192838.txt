PART 9 — TITLE & HISTORY FLAGS MODULE

IMPORTANT:
Do not turn RideCheck into CARFAX, a DMV system, title agency, or law-enforcement verification platform.

RideCheck remains:
- a Vehicle Transparency Platform
- an observational inspection system
- a documentation and risk-awareness platform

RideCheck does NOT:
- guarantee title validity
- run title databases
- certify flood history
- certify theft status
- certify accident history
- guarantee odometer accuracy

Those services remain optional buyer-paid upsells such as:
- CARFAX
- AutoCheck
- NMVTIS
- professional title search services

This module documents observable indicators only.

Do not change the existing inspection flow.
Add this module after the OBD-II module.

────────────────────────────
MODULE:
TITLE & HISTORY FLAGS
(Where Available)
────────────────────────────

This module is optional and observational.

Purpose:
Document visible title, VIN, flood, tampering, odometer, and accident-repair indicators observed during inspection.

The module should gracefully support:
- private-party transactions
- dealership transactions
- situations where title is unavailable
- situations where seller refuses title review

Do not require title review to complete inspection.

────────────────────────────
REPORT INTEGRATION
────────────────────────────

Add section:

TITLE & HISTORY FLAGS

Place after:
OBD-II DIAGNOSTIC DATA

Use same report philosophy as all RideCheck sections:

OBSERVED:
Only factual observations documented by RideChecker.

CONSIDERATION:
Neutral explanation of what indicators may typically mean.

Examples:

TITLE STATUS
OBSERVED:
Physical title reviewed. Salvage branding observed.

CONSIDERATION:
Salvage or rebuilt branding may affect financing, resale value, insurance eligibility, and registration requirements. Independent verification recommended.

FLOOD/WATER INTRUSION
OBSERVED:
Corrosion inside cabin areas and musty odor observed.

CONSIDERATION:
Indicators consistent with possible prior water intrusion were observed. Water exposure can affect electrical systems and interior materials over time. Professional assessment recommended.

PRIOR ACCIDENT/REPAIR
OBSERVED:
Overspray and inconsistent panel gaps observed.

CONSIDERATION:
Indicators consistent with prior repair work were observed. Additional body or structural assessment may provide further clarity.

THEFT/TAMPERING
OBSERVED:
VIN plate appeared damaged.

CONSIDERATION:
VIN irregularities should be independently verified before transaction completion.

────────────────────────────
MISSING OR LIMITED INFORMATION
────────────────────────────

If title unavailable:
Add:
"Physical title was not available for review at time of inspection. Independent title verification recommended."

If lien noted with no release:
Add:
"Lien noted during inspection. No lien release document was present at time of inspection."

If VIN mismatch:
Add:
"VIN discrepancy observed during inspection. Immediate independent verification recommended before transaction completion."

Do NOT say:
- stolen vehicle
- flood car
- title fraud
- rollback confirmed
- seller hiding something

Use only:
- indicators observed
- discrepancy observed
- unable to verify
- professional verification recommended
- independent verification recommended

────────────────────────────
INSPECTION CONFIDENCE IMPACT
────────────────────────────

Increase confidence when:
- title reviewed
- VINs matched
- no major indicators observed

Lower confidence when:
- title unavailable
- VIN unverified
- flood indicators observed
- odometer inconsistency observed
- multiple tampering indicators observed

Confidence reflects inspection completeness only.
Not vehicle quality.

────────────────────────────
UPSELL POSITIONING
────────────────────────────

Do NOT automatically include CARFAX or title reports.

If buyer purchased:
- CARFAX
- AutoCheck
- title history add-on

Then integrate those results into separate paid-add-on sections.

Otherwise:
RideCheck only documents physical observable indicators.

RideCheck should not become dependent on third-party history providers.
