---

PROBLEM

RideCheck cannot reliably initiate a Messenger conversation with an unconnected Facebook Marketplace seller.

Therefore, when a buyer submits a Facebook Marketplace listing, RideCheck must not offer seller contact in a way that implies RideCheck will initiate that Messenger conversation.

This implementation is specifically about the INITIAL Facebook Marketplace seller contact.

Once the buyer has initiated contact and the seller agrees, RideCheck may continue through the existing seller-coordination workflow using whatever contact information the seller provides.

---

PRODUCT RULE

NON-FACEBOOK LISTINGS

Preserve the existing choices:

1. I will contact the seller
2. RideCheck Concierge will contact the seller

Do not change this working flow.

---

FACEBOOK MARKETPLACE LISTINGS

When the listing URL/source is reliably identified as Facebook Marketplace:

Disable the normal:

“RideCheck Concierge will contact the seller”

initial-contact option.

Replace it with:

FACEBOOK MARKETPLACE SELLER CONTACT

“Facebook requires you to initiate contact with the seller.

RideCheck will provide the message and take over coordination once the seller agrees.”

Display the prewritten seller message:

“Hi, I'm interested in your vehicle and would like to have an independent pre-purchase inspection completed through RideCheck. There is no cost to you as the seller. Are you okay with RideCheck inspecting the vehicle before I make my decision?”

Actions:

[Copy Seller Message]

[Open Facebook Listing]

After copying, show:

“Send this message to the seller through Facebook Messenger. Once the seller agrees, return here to continue.”

Then provide:

[Seller Agreed — Continue]

---

SELLER AGREED FLOW

IMPORTANT:

“Seller Agreed” means the BUYER IS REPORTING that the seller has agreed.

It does NOT automatically mean RideCheck has independently confirmed the seller, inspection appointment, date, time, or location.

Do not automatically convert buyer-reported consent into an existing authoritative “seller confirmed” status if that status currently means RideCheck/Ops has independently confirmed arrangements.

Store buyer-reported consent separately where necessary.

When the buyer selects:

[Seller Agreed — Continue]

Ask for any information the seller provided:

- Seller name — optional
- Seller phone — optional
- Seller email — optional
- Inspection address/location — optional
- Seller availability/date/time — optional

Do not require information the buyer does not yet have.

The buyer must be able to continue even if the seller only said “yes” and has not yet provided phone, email, address, or availability.

Save available information into existing authoritative seller/order fields where appropriate.

Do not create duplicate seller fields if equivalent fields already exist.

Then transition the order into the EXISTING RideCheck seller-coordination workflow.

RideCheck can subsequently coordinate using seller-provided contact information.

Do not create a second seller communication system.
