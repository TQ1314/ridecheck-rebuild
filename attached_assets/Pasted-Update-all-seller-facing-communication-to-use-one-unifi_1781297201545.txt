Update all seller-facing communication to use one unified conversion-focused messaging system.

Goal:
Every seller-facing message — SMS, email, Craigslist relay email, dealership email, roadside message, and future WhatsApp/message templates — must follow the same RideCheck persuasion framework.

We are not just contacting sellers. We are trying to win seller cooperation.

Create a centralized seller messaging module:
lib/seller-contact/sellerMessaging.ts

It should generate:
- SMS body
- email subject
- email plain text
- email branded HTML
- short follow-up variants

Use the same core message logic across all channels.

SELLER CONVERSION FRAMEWORK:

Every seller message should communicate:

1. Serious buyer
“The buyer is serious and has already requested a RideCheck inspection.”

2. Low friction
“The inspection is brief, usually 30–45 minutes.”

3. Convenience
“We come to you / your lot / where the vehicle is parked.”

4. Non-invasive
“No repairs, no disassembly, no modifications.”

5. Trust
“RideCheck is an independent vehicle inspection service.”

6. Benefit to seller
“Confirming the inspection helps serious buyers move forward with confidence.”

7. Clear CTA
“Reply with a time that works.”

SOURCE-AWARE VARIANTS:

Online Marketplace / Listing:
- Reference the vehicle/listing.
- Do not sound accusatory.
- Emphasize serious buyer and quick inspection.

Dealership:
- Use dealership language.
- “A customer is interested in a vehicle on your lot.”
- “We can work around your business hours.”
- Avoid private seller wording.

Roadside / For Sale Sign:
- “I’m reaching out about the vehicle with the For Sale sign.”
- “A buyer would like to arrange an independent inspection.”
- Avoid marketplace/listing language.

TONE:
- Professional
- Warm
- Clear
- Non-threatening
- Seller-friendly
- No scam/fraud language
- No “red flag” language
- No aggressive pressure
- No accusations

SMS TEMPLATE EXAMPLE:
“Hi, this is RideCheck. A serious buyer requested a quick 30–45 min inspection for your [vehicle]. We come to you and do not perform repairs or take anything apart. What time works for you?”

EMAIL TEMPLATE STRUCTURE:
Subject:
“Inspection request for your [vehicle] — RideCheck”

Body:
- Greeting
- Serious buyer has requested inspection
- What RideCheck does
- Inspection is brief and non-invasive
- Seller convenience
- CTA: reply with availability
- RideCheck signature and support info

FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE:
Attempt 1:
Polite intro / serious buyer / schedule request

Attempt 2:
Short reminder / buyer still interested / flexible timing

Attempt 3:
Final follow-up / if no reply, buyer will be advised that seller was unreachable

IMPORTANT:
Attempt 3 should be firm but still professional.
Do not say seller refused unless they explicitly refused.
Use “unreachable” or “unable to confirm availability.”

DELIVERY CHANNEL RULE:
The core message should remain consistent across SMS and email.
Email can be longer and branded.
SMS should be shorter but must carry the same persuasion points.

UI:
In SellerContactPanel, label templates clearly:
- Initial outreach
- Follow-up
- Final follow-up

Ops should be able to review/edit before sending.

Do not break:
- existing seller contact history
- delivery tracking
- Resend webhook
- Twilio SMS
- source-aware templates
- email validation
- report safety controls
